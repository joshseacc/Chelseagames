✨ 練習 1：辨析時間先後 (Time Order)

🎒 技巧工具包 (Skill Toolkit)

  • 🕰️ 留意時間範圍[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span)
    Pay attention to time periods.
  • ☀️ 一天內的時間：「早上」➔「下午」➔「晚上」[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span)
    Times of a day: Morning ➔ Afternoon ➔ Night.
  • 📜 跨度大的時間：「遠古」➔「秦朝」➔「現代」[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span)
    Historical times: Ancient ➔ Dynasties ➔ Modern.
  • 🌅 暗藏的時間詞：「日出」(早上)、「晚霞」(傍晚)[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span)
    Hidden time words: Sunrise (Morning), Sunset (Evening).

📜 經典範例 (Classic Example)

A. 遠古時期，古人用貝殼當貨幣[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span)

🪄 解題魔法 (Magic Steps)

  1. 找時間 (Find time): 「遠古時期」最早 ➔ A 為首句[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span)。
  2. 排順序 (Order): 秦朝早過宋朝 ➔ A ➔ B ➔ D[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span)。
  3. 配對 (Match): C 的「貝」連住 A[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span)；E 的「銅錢」連住 B[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span)。
  4. 答案 (Answer): A ➔ C ➔ B ➔ E ➔ D[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span)

✨ 練習 2：找出代詞的指代對象 (Pronoun Reference)

🎒 技巧工具包 (Skill Toolkit)

  • 👉 找出代詞：「他」、「那」、「它」[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span)
    Spot pronouns like He, That, It.
  • 🔍 代詞短語：「這種現象」、「這個地方」[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span)
    Look for phrases like "This phenomenon", "This place".
  • 🚫 代詞不放頭：有代詞的句子通常不會是第一句[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span)。
    Sentences with pronouns are NOT the first sentence.
  • 🔗 緊隨其後：代詞句一定跟在「真正主角」後面[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span)。
    Pronouns always follow their target object.

📜 經典範例 (Classic Example)

A. 這種現象就是極光[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span)
B. 它是太陽風擾動地球磁場[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span)
C. 擾動期間能量釋放時產生的光芒[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span)
D. 光帶互相交織在一起，直到點亮整片夜空[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span)
E. 在北極或南極，有時夜晚會出現一些彩色光帶[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span)

🪄 解題魔法 (Magic Steps)

  1. 找背景 (Find background): E 介紹地點和顏色 ➔ E 為首句[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span)。
  2. 找代詞 (Find pronouns): A 的「這種現象」指 D 的光帶 ➔ D ➔ A[span_24](start_span)[span_24](end_span)。
    B 的「它」指 A 的極光 ➔ A ➔ B ➔ C[span_25](start_span)[span_25](end_span)。
  3. 答案 (Answer): E ➔ D ➔ A ➔ B ➔ C[span_26](start_span)[span_26](end_span)

✨ 練習 3：尋找對應事物 (Matching Objects)

🎒 技巧工具包 (Skill Toolkit)

  • 🔁 重複的主角：一直出現的事物就是主角！[span_27](start_span)[span_27](end_span)
    Repeated objects are the main characters!
  • 🤝 句意相連：上一句的結尾 ＝ 下一句的開頭[span_28](start_span)[span_28](end_span)。
    End of sentence 1 = Start of sentence 2.
  • 🧩 合體變長句：把意思連貫的短句拼成一句來讀[span_29](start_span)[span_29](end_span)。
    Combine connected sentences to read them easily.

📜 經典範例 (Classic Example)

A. 蟋蟀們在草叢中高聲合唱[span_30](start_span)[span_30](end_span)
B. 夏天的夜晚，小池塘熱鬧得很[span_31](start_span)[span_31](end_span)
C. 牠們將池塘變成了音樂會場館[span_32](start_span)[span_32](end_span)
D. 魚兒在水中游來游去，似是在為牠們伴舞[span_33](start_span)[span_33](end_span)
E. 聽見蟋蟀的歌聲，青蛙也決定要大展歌喉[span_34](start_span)[span_34](end_span)

🪄 解題魔法 (Magic Steps)

  1. 找背景 (Find background): B 交代時間地點 ➔ B 為首句[span_35](start_span)[span_35](end_span)。
  2. 找重複 (Find repeats): A 講「蟋蟀」，E 講聽到「蟋蟀」唱歌 ➔ A ➔ E[span_36](start_span)[span_36](end_span)。
  3. 排結局 (Order ending): C 總結變成音樂會 ➔ C 為尾句[span_37](start_span)[span_37](end_span)。D 講「伴舞」跟在唱歌後 ➔ E ➔ D[span_38](start_span)[span_38](end_span)。
  4. 答案 (Answer): B ➔ A ➔ E ➔ D ➔ C[span_39](start_span)[span_39](end_span)

✨ 練習 4：尋找首尾句 (Head & Tail Sentences)

🎒 技巧工具包 (Skill Toolkit)

  • 👑 找首句 (Head)：交代背景、下定義、帶出話題[span_40](start_span)[span_40](end_span)。

  • 🏁 找尾句 (Tail)：總結全文、抒發感情[span_41](start_span)[span_41](end_span)。
    Tail sentence concludes or expresses feelings.

📜 經典範例 (Classic Example)

🪄 解題魔法 (Magic Steps)

  1. 定頭尾 (Head & Tail): B 帶出「香蕉」話題 ➔ B 為首句[span_47](start_span)[span_47](end_span)。E 總結優點 ➔ E 為尾句[span_48](start_span)[span_48](end_span)。
  2. 排中間 (Middle): C 有「它」接住 B ➔ B ➔ C[span_49](start_span)[span_49](end_span)。A 講營養，D 解釋營養功能 ➔ A ➔ D[span_50](start_span)[span_50](end_span)。
  3. 答案 (Answer): B ➔ C ➔ A ➔ D ➔ E[span_51](start_span)[span_51](end_span)

✨ 練習 5：找出中心句 (Main Idea Sentence)

🎒 技巧工具包 (Skill Toolkit)

  • 🎯 概括全段：中心句就是整段的「大意」[span_52](start_span)[span_52](end_span)。
    Main idea sentence summarizes the whole paragraph.
  • 📍 位置在哪？：通常在第一句 (總領) 或 最後一句 (總結)[span_53](start_span)[span_53](end_span)。
    Usually at the Very Beginning or the Very End.
  • 🚩 標示字眼：見到「總而言之」、「由此可見」➔ 尾句！[span_54](start_span)[span_54](end_span)
    Clues: "In conclusion" ➔ Tail sentence!

B. 人們為了生存和追求商業利潤[span_56](start_span)[span_56](end_span)
C. 以致森林面積減少了約百分之十七[span_57](start_span)[span_57](end_span)
D. 「地球之肺」亞馬遜雨林的現況正岌岌可危[span_58](start_span)[span_58](end_span)
E. 若情況沒有得到抑制，亞馬遜雨林可能會完全消失[span_59](start_span)[span_59](end_span)

🪄 解題魔法 (Magic Steps)

  1. 找中心句 (Main Idea): D 點出情況嚴峻 ➔ D 為首句[span_60](start_span)[span_60](end_span)。
  2. 定尾句 (Tail): E 說出最壞後果 ➔ E 為尾句[span_61](start_span)[span_61](end_span)。
  3. 排中間 (Middle): B「為了…」接 A「砍伐樹木」➔ B ➔ A[span_62](start_span)[span_62](end_span)。C「以致…」接在破壞行為之後 ➔ A ➔ C[span_63](start_span)[span_63](end_span)。
  4. 答案 (Answer): D ➔ B ➔ A ➔ C ➔ E[span_64](start_span)[span_64](end_span)

✨ 練習 6：辨別關聯詞 (Connecting Words)

🎒 技巧工具包 (Skill Toolkit)

  • 🔗 好拍檔：「只要…就…」、「除非…才…」[span_65](start_span)[span_65](end_span)。
    Word pairs: "As long as... then...", "Unless... only...".
  • 👀 注意配對：有時「就」、「也」可以配不同字，要看前文後理！[span_66](start_span)[span_66](end_span)
    Some words can match multiple partners, check the logic!

📜 經典範例 (Classic Example)

A. 乘客才有可能受傷[span_67](start_span)[span_67](end_span)

D. 除非車內的安全氣囊系統受損[span_70](start_span)[span_70](end_span)
E. 銷售員不停地強調這輛汽車很安全[span_71](start_span)[span_71](end_span)

🪄 解題魔法 (Magic Steps)

  1. 找背景 (Find background): E 帶出推銷汽車 ➔ E 為首句[span_72](start_span)[span_72](end_span)。
  2. 配對關聯詞 (Match connectors): 「就算 (C)」配「也 (B)」➔ C ➔ B[span_73](start_span)[span_73](end_span)。「除非 (D)」配「才 (A)」➔ D ➔ A[span_74](start_span)[span_74](end_span)。
